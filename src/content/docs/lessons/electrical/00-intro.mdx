# **Welcome to the Electrical Subteam!**

We wire and troubleshoot the systems that **power, control, and connect the robot** — from the battery to motors and sensors.

## **What You'll Learn**

* Electrical safety and good wiring practices
* How power flows through an FRC robot
* The **new 2027 SystemCore control system**
* Power distribution & circuit breakers
* Motors and motor controllers
* CAN communication
* Sensors
* Crimping, soldering, and cable management
* Multimeter use and troubleshooting

## **General Resources**

### **1. FRC Electrical System**

Learn the major components and how they connect.

→ [2027 SystemCore Introduction](https://docs.wpilib.org/en/2027/docs/software/systemcore-info/systemcore-introduction.html)\

### **2. Power**

Battery → main breaker → power distribution → robot devices.

Learn wire gauge, breakers, voltage drop, shorts, and brownouts.

→ [REV Power Distribution Hub](https://docs.revrobotics.com/ion-control/pdh/overview)\
→ [PDH Wiring Guide](https://docs.revrobotics.com/ion-control/pdh/gs/wiring)\
→ [Brownouts & Current Draw](https://docs.wpilib.org/en/2027/docs/software/systemcore-info/roborio-brownouts.html)

### **3. Motors & Motor Controllers**

Learn how motors receive power and commands, and how our two main motor-control systems differ.



**REV SPARK MAX**

* Separate motor controller
* Power input → SPARK MAX → motor
* CAN connection for control and configuration

→ [SPARK MAX Overview](https://docs.revrobotics.com/brushless/spark-max/overview)\
→ [SPARK MAX Wiring](https://docs.revrobotics.com/brushless/spark-max/gs/wiring)\
→ [SPARK MAX Control Interfaces](https://docs.revrobotics.com/brushless/spark-max/control-interfaces)



**CTRE Talon FX / Kraken**

* Talon FX controller is integrated into the Kraken motor
* Power and CAN connect directly to the motor/controller assembly
* Configured using Phoenix Tuner X

→ [Talon FX / Kraken Overview](https://v6.docs.ctr-electronics.com/en/stable/docs/hardware-reference/talonfx/index.html)\
→ [Phoenix Tuner X](https://v6.docs.ctr-electronics.com/en/stable/docs/tuner/index.html)
