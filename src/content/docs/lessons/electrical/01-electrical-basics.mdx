---
title: 01-basic concepts
---
In this first session, we'll cover the basic electrical concepts and safety skills you need before working on the robot. You'll also get hands-on practice using a multimeter and troubleshooting simple wiring problems.

**Agenda:**

* Basic electrical concepts: voltage, current, resistance, and power
* FRC electrical safety: polarity, shorts, breakers, and wire gauge
* How to use a multimeter
* Activity: measure voltage, test continuity, and diagnose a simple wiring fault
